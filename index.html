    What it is

    A heads-up awareness overlay for recreational airsoft and casual play with friends. It shows heading, your position, squad mates, waypoints, rooms you marked clear, and people/animals the camera has confirmed. No scoring, nothing uploaded.

    Start

    Tap START. Allow camera, then Motion & Orientation (needed for compass and step counting), then location. If you denied something: tap aA → Website Settings, or Settings › Apps › Safari (Camera, Microphone, Location, Motion & Orientation Access), then reload.

    Squad over a hotspot (no internet)

    1. One phone turns on Personal Hotspot; others join it (or all join the same Wi-Fi). 2. Everyone opens PIGEON EYE and taps START (camera permission also lets Safari use local network addresses). 3. Host: SQUAD → HOST: NEW OFFER (QR appears). 4. Joiner: JOIN: SCAN OFFER → point at host's QR → an answer QR appears. 5. Host: HOST: SCAN ANSWER → point at joiner's QR. Link shows "direct" with latency. Repeat 3–5 for each member (host relays to everyone, 2–6 phones). 6. Stand together and tap SYNC START POINT.

    If pairing never connects: Safari may hide local IPs behind mDNS names that some hotspots don't resolve. Try the hotspot phone as host, make sure every phone granted camera permission, keep screens on during pairing, or use Online mode when you have signal.

    Compass / GPS not working on iPhone?

    Tap ENABLE COMPASS (or SENSORS › ENABLE) and choose Allow. If no prompt appears: Settings › Apps › Safari › Motion & Orientation Access on; for location: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services on, Safari Websites (or the Home Screen app) → While Using + Precise Location on. Compass heading also needs Location Services › System Services › Compass Calibration. A Home Screen app keeps its own permissions; if it still runs an old version, delete the icon and add it again.

    Calibration

    FOV: stand a friend of known height at a measured distance (e.g. 10 m), wait for the confirmed box, enter the distance and tap CALIBRATE. Step length: tap START STEP CAL, walk a measured distance normally, enter it, tap STOP & SET.

    Positioning

    GPS mode when the fix is ≤15 m accurate; otherwise PDR (pedestrian dead reckoning: steps × step length along the compass). PDR drifts, typically 5–10 % of the distance walked, and heading errors bend the path. The accuracy figure grows as you walk. Good GPS fixes pull the estimate back.

    Limitations

    Distances (~) assume a standing adult of the set height and a calibrated FOV. They're wrong for crouching, partly hidden or cut-off people (shown as ≤). Bearings depend on the compass, which is disturbed near metal, cars, rebar, magnets and replica bodies; recalibrate by moving the phone in a figure-8. Squad mates are not recognised by the camera; their markers come from the positions they share. Detection is COCO "person"/animals only. Foreground only: the screen must stay on and the app open; pauses are logged as gaps.

    Offline

    Open once with internet and Add to Home Screen. The app shell, libraries and detector are cached for use with no signal (status on the start screen). Sound classification downloads its model only if enabled.
